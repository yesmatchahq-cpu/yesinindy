Privacy Policy

YES Volleyball app. Run by Yes Matcha LLC, Indianapolis, Indiana. Last updated November 13, 2026.

What we collect

  • Phone number. Used only to sign you in with a text code. Other players never see it.
  • Profile. Your first and last name, optional photo, skill level, positions and area. Other players see your first name, last initial, photo, level and positions.
  • What you do in the app. Games you mark going, maybe or waitlist, teams and squads you join, friend requests, posts when you need a player, and whether you are free to play.
  • Messages. Group chats and direct messages, including photos and reactions you send. Only people in that chat can see them. Group chats delete themselves when they close.
  • Notifications. A push token from your phone so we can send game reminders and messages. You can turn these off in Settings.
  • Payments. When you pay for a game, Square handles your card. We never see or store your card number. We keep a record that you paid.
  • Reports. If you report someone, we keep the report so we can review it.

How we use it

To run the app: sign you in, show you games, let you chat and play with others, send reminders, take payments and keep the community safe. We do not sell your data, use it for ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the app: Supabase (database and storage), Twilio (sign in texts), Expo (app updates and notifications), Apple (notifications) and Square (payments). They may only use your data to provide their service to us.

Safety

You can block anyone. Blocking hides you from each other everywhere. Reports go to us and are reviewed, and accounts with repeated reports are hidden or removed. YES Volleyball is for players 13 and older.

Deleting your data

Delete your account anytime from the profile menu in the app (tap your picture at the top left). Your profile, games, chats, messages and photos are removed. Payment records may be kept as required for taxes.
